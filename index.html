01 / The problem

Growth exposes weak systems.

Many smaller manufacturers do not need a massive software transformation. They need the information they already have to become easier to trust, find, and use.

01

Too many spreadsheets

Critical information lives across files owned by different people, with inconsistent formats and no clear source of truth.

02

Manual work everywhere

Teams spend time copying values, checking fields, sending updates, and rebuilding the same reports over and over.

03

Decisions come late

When data is difficult to combine or validate, managers get visibility after the problem has already happened.

02 / What Metriva builds

Practical systems before expensive software.

We start with the operational problem and build the smallest useful system around it. The goal is not more technology. The goal is less friction.

A

Operational Databases

Move important business information out of fragile spreadsheet chains and into a structured system your team can maintain.

  • Data model and database design
  • Import and cleanup of existing data
  • Validation rules and controlled fields
  • Simple interfaces built around the workflow
B

Reporting & Visibility

Turn operational data into clear reporting so your team can see what needs attention without rebuilding reports manually.

  • Management dashboards
  • Exception and status reporting
  • Automated recurring reports
  • Operational KPI tracking
C

Workflow Automation

Remove repetitive steps where people are moving the same information between files, systems, emails, or teams.

  • Spreadsheet and file automation
  • Alerts and validation checks
  • Data movement between systems
  • Repeatable review workflows
D

Systems Roadmap

When the current setup has reached its limit, we map what should be improved now and what can wait until the business is ready to scale further.

  • Current workflow review
  • Data and process mapping
  • Priority recommendations
  • Path toward cloud and larger systems

03 / Who it is for

Built for the stage before enterprise software makes sense.

Metriva is a fit when your business has real operational complexity but the answer is not yet another huge implementation project.

Good fit

You are likely ready for Metriva if:

  • Important processes depend on Excel or shared files
  • People copy the same data into multiple places
  • Reporting takes hours to prepare
  • Errors are found through manual checking
  • You want a better system without replacing everything

Not trying to be everything.

Metriva is not a replacement for every ERP, MES, or accounting platform. We focus on the gaps around those systems: the manual workflows, disconnected information, reporting problems, and processes that have outgrown their current setup.

Start with one painful process. Fix it properly. Prove the value. Then expand.

04 / How it works

A simple path from problem to working system.

No giant discovery phase. We first understand the workflow, identify the highest value bottleneck, and build around what your team actually does.

01

Review

Walk through the process, files, systems, and recurring pain points with the people doing the work.

02

Map

Define the information flow, business rules, duplicated work, and the smallest useful target solution.

03

Build

Create the database, reporting, automation, or lightweight application needed to solve the problem.

04

Improve

Use real feedback from the workflow to refine the system and decide what is worth automating next.

05 / Why Metriva

Understand the operation before touching the technology.

The best system is not the one with the most features. It is the one your team understands, trusts, and actually uses.

That means learning how the work happens today, where information breaks down, what creates delays, and which problems are worth solving first.

Metriva is intentionally starting narrow: data, reporting, and workflow systems for smaller manufacturers. That focus keeps the work practical and close to measurable operational problems.
