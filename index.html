The approach

Understand the process before touching the technology.

Most manufacturing teams do not need another platform just because a process is messy. First, we map what actually happens today: where the data starts, who touches it, what gets checked, and where the delays show up.

01

Bring one real workflow

“We rebuild this every Monday.”

That is enough to start. A report, tracker, product-data review, inventory reconciliation, purchasing check, or any recurring process that depends on exports, spreadsheets, and manual follow-up.

02

Map the current state

Count the steps.

We document the source files, copy/paste steps, checks, handoffs, and recurring exceptions before recommending anything.

03

Pick a useful measure

No made-up ROI.

Track something we can actually observe: minutes per run, number of manual handoffs, fields checked, or exceptions that need follow-up.

04

Build only what earns its place

Automate the repetitive part. Keep people where judgment matters.

The result might be a cleaner report, validation logic, a small database, an alert, or a lightweight internal tool. If the best answer is to leave part of the process manual, we say that too.

What we build

Practical fixes around the systems you already use.

01

Reporting that does not need rebuilding

Recurring operational reports, status views, KPI summaries, and exception lists built from the data your team already produces.

02

Checks that catch the boring mistakes

Validation rules for missing values, mismatches, duplicate records, date logic, status rules, and other repeatable checks people should not have to do by eye.

03

Cleaner data between ERP and Excel

Structured imports, standardized fields, controlled values, and repeatable cleanup for CSV or Excel exports coming from ERP and operational systems.

04

Small internal tools for awkward workflows

When a spreadsheet is doing too much, we can replace the fragile part with a simple database, interface, or workflow tool without replacing the ERP behind it.
